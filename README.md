# McDonald's vs. Starbucks: Financial Statement Analysis
 
A FIN410 (Financial Statement Analysis, Section 4) group report from North South University comparing the financial health, valuation and earnings quality of **McDonald's** and **Starbucks** over 2022-2024.
 
## Summary
 
The report covers income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and OCI analysis, ratio benchmarking (profitability, liquidity, efficiency, solvency, market), DCF valuation and earnings quality.
 
- **McDonald's:** asset-light franchise model with very high margins and strong cash flow, but high leverage and negative equity from buybacks.
- **Starbucks:** company-operated model with thinner margins, but stronger liquidity, higher asset turnover and a more conservative capital structure.
Both companies show strong earnings quality, with operating cash flow exceeding net income.
 

 
*Prepared for academic purposes only. Not investment advice.*
